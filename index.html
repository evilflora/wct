Warframe Completion Tracker

Track your Warframe progress

Warframe Completion Tracker helps you keep track of everything you've collected and everything left to unlock in Warframe: Warframes, Primary Weapons, Secondary Weapons, Melee Weapons, Companions, Archwings, Mods, Arcanes, Honorifics, Mastery Rank progress and more.

  • Warframes & Prime variants
  • Primary, Secondary & Melee weapons
  • Companions & Archwings
  • Mods & Arcanes
  • Mastery Rank tracking

All progress is saved locally in your browser, with optional export/import and cross-device sync.

[♟] Sync

Explanations

You can synchronise your progression across multiple browsers and devices on the GitHub url (does not work without web server / http server).

If you try to sync data from a higher version to an older one, it will locally strip non-existing items.

It works by using Google Firebase and a unique ID that can be generated below.

Usage

By pressing "CREATE", a random secret key will be generated, do not lose it (it'll be included in the export file).

If you already have a secret key, you can paste it and then SYNC, this allow you to download or upload your save.

Keep this secret, do not share, do not show it. Anyone with this key will be able to modify your save.

    Welcome to Warframe Completion Tracker

    What is this?

    This tool lets you track (almost) everything you've obtained in Warframe: Warframes, weapons, mods, companions, arcanes, honorias, ... that were freely accessible through login or gameplay.

    So, no Twitch rewards, no redeem codes, no console exclusives, no Platinum-only market items, and no rewards from the Warframe website.

    Please remember, data may be incorrect or not up to date. Discussion area on GitHub.

    How it works

    Click on any item to mark it as owned. Your progress is saved automatically in this browser's local storage, nothing is sent anywhere unless you use SYNC.

    Do not clear the cache of the browser, or you will lose everything (unless you exported it or synced it).

    Main features

    [♟] SYNC synchronise your progress across devices using a secret key.
